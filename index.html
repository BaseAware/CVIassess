Violent Critical Incident Readiness Assessment

This evidence-based readiness assessment produces two scores. School Readiness covers what the school controls. Public Safety Readiness covers what police, fire, EMS and dispatch control. Each sub-item is rated 0–10 against written anchors, then weighted by how it was confirmed: verified on site, documented, or self-reported. It is not a predictor of violence.

You are viewing a sample assessment with example ratings. It is not a real school.

Nine domains the school or district can fix. Prevention 30%, protection 50%, response 15%, recovery 5%.

Six domains scored with the police, fire, EMS and dispatch agencies that serve the campus. They never lower the School Readiness score.
